## About

RotaSphere is the official website development and maintenance project of the Rotaract Club of the University of Ruhuna. It gives the club one professional digital home for its story, projects, events, and partnerships, and is maintained by a dedicated team of student developers.

**Status:** Coming soon

## Vision

To build a connected, transparent, and accessible digital home for the Rotaract Club of the University of Ruhuna that amplifies its service, strengthens its partnerships, and preserves its legacy for future generations.

## Mission

To design, develop, and continuously maintain the club's official website through collaborative student expertise, ensuring reliable, secure, and engaging digital experiences for members, partners, and the community.

## Objectives

- Launch an official, professional website for the club
- Showcase projects, events, and impact reports in one place
- Provide a reliable channel for partners and sponsors
- Establish a maintenance framework to keep the site secure and up to date
- Preserve the club's institutional knowledge for future boards

## Key Features (Planned)

- Home and club profile
- Projects showcase organised by avenue
- Events and news
- Media gallery
- Partner and sponsor hub
- Admin dashboard for authorised board members
- Responsive, fast, SEO-friendly design
